Before You Apply

Simple admission checklist for students

Check these points before paying fees or confirming your college seat.

01

Check Course Eligibility

Make sure your marks, subjects and eligibility match the course.

02

Verify College Approval

Check the university affiliation, accreditation and applicable approvals.

03

Compare Total Fees

Check tuition, hostel, transport and other charges before deciding.

04

Check Placement Information

Look at official placement information, recruiters and internship opportunities.

05

Understand Admission Route

Check whether admission is through counselling, entrance exam or another route.

06

Keep Documents Ready

Keep marksheets, ID proof, certificates and photographs ready before applying.
